Why BE?

Education is a fundamental human right and the greatest equaliser.

Our mission is to create a global community of people and, together, bring education to children by building schools and securing access to creativity.

Bella Esperanza | BE is an Italian non-profit organisation building a global charity movement for the new generation. It was founded in 2023, inspired by Alexandra Pfeifer’s volunteering experiences in Tanzania.

Our first pilot project is in Tanzania, where we are building our school in Ugwachanya.

Our promise to every child: become who you were born to BE.

Our (love) story

It is pure love. From the beginning to infinity.

In 2017 our founder Alexandra met Esperanza, a 2-year-old girl, at the Tosamaganga orphan centre in Tanzania. That bond turned into a mission.

Their bond gave life to a mission: to provide education to children like Esperanza, who face an uncertain future after having to leave the orphanage at the age of six and return to relatives who are often unable to care for them properly.

Alexandra promised Esperanza she would get the chance to go to school. That promise became the foundation of Bella Esperanza | BE.

In 2023 Esperanza started attending the first school BE is building — the promise was kept.

In Spanish, Esperanza means hope. At Bella Esperanza we do not only believe in hope: we live it. And we care about keeping our promises.

Why education?

We are in a global education crisis.

Education is more than a right: it is the foundation for opportunity, equality and hope. Yet millions of children are deprived of this essential tool for a better future.

Right now there are

260

million children worldwide who do not go to school.

If we do not act now

825

million young people will lack the skills needed to be an adequate workforce in 2030.

That is

10%

of the world population. 1 in 10 of us.

The new generations are our future and tomorrow’s leaders. Education is power.

01

Drives economic growth

Supporting early learning is the best investment a government can make, returning $17 for every $1 invested in early childhood.

02

Empowers future generations

Investing in education today lays the foundation for future leaders and innovators, ensuring long-term progress and resilience.

03

International cooperation

Education fosters a deeper understanding of global issues, different cultures and different perspectives.

04

Less poverty and child labour

Skills and knowledge create better job opportunities, which can lift entire families and communities out of poverty.

05

Reduced migration

Education reduces conflict and migration by fostering understanding, creating stability and offering opportunities close to home.

06

Better health

Education raises awareness of health issues, lowering mortality rates and improving general health.

07

Gender equality

Access to education, especially for girls, enables women to take full part in economic, social and political life.

08

Climate change

Education builds the awareness and skills needed for sustainable practices, addressing critical issues such as resource conservation.

Our school

A transformative start in Ugwachanya, Tanzania.

The first primary school we are building will give 360 children access to education by 2027.

Since 2021 we have been working with the Province of Bolzano, an Italian non-profit organisation and local partners in Tanzania on our first pilot project in Ugwachanya. This initiative marks the beginning of our journey to bring education and opportunity to underserved communities.

The school, run and administered by our local partners, the Teresine Sisters, is a concrete example of the power of collaboration. It offers not only education, but the chance of a better future for every one of our students.

It is carefully designed to support both learning and wellbeing.

What the school includes

  • 10 Classrooms
  • 12 Bathrooms
  • 2 Dormitories for 100 children
  • 3 Offices
  • 1 Kitchen
  • 1 Canteen
  • 1 Laundry
  • 1 Teachers’ house
  • 1 Sports field
  • 1 Administration building
  • 1 Library

How we work

The Charity 3C model

Our goal is to build an innovative charity project for a new generation. All our work rests on three principles.

C

Credible

We are committed to transparency and trust. Every cent of your online donation goes entirely to building the school. We guarantee this through our two-bank-account system.

C

Collaborative

We work with local experts to build our school project. Our partners have years of experience and deep knowledge of how to deliver school projects in their area.

C

Contemporary

We believe in the power of brands to inspire change. That is why we are building a contemporary brand that carries messages of hope, not pity, for the new generation.

Our values

We believe in empowering communities through innovation and creativity, championing diversity and staying true to our mission.

Collaboration

We build partnerships with individuals, companies, brands and communities to widen our impact together.

Kindness

We believe in the transformative power of compassion: everyone should feel valued and inspired to take part.

Inclusion

We make sure everyone has access to opportunity and feels welcome in our community.

Respect

We respect the dignity, rights and voices of every child and every community we serve.

Innovation

We look for creative solutions to the challenges of education and charity in the modern world.

Diversity

We value unique perspectives and celebrate diversity as a source of strength and inspiration.

Authenticity

We value honesty, transparency and staying true to our roots.

Transparency

Two accounts. No doubt about where your money goes.

We keep two separate bank accounts: one for the school project and one for operating costs, which are covered by a group of generous private donors. Your online donation goes entirely to the school project.
